# A Separate Project Developed to Complement AIJMC

It is planned that if my graduation project is approved and I have the chance to join the graduation thesis, then I will consider researching this project.

AIJMC aims to be a platform where people can find jobs in the IT industry (Planning to expand into multiple industries). However, there is a concerning reality as AI develops. AIJMC records quite a lot of job postings, with 2/3 of them considered reliable, but 1/3 of them have many irregularities such as AI-generated jobs, risk of fraud or low credibility.

To address this problem, JPRA is being researched and developed.
